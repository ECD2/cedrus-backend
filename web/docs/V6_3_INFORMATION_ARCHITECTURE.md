# Cedrus V6.3 information architecture

## The World

The opening viewport remains a low-information ambient environment. It communicates Miami time, the current planning sentence, schedule pressure through terrain illumination, and the existence of more depth through a quiet scroll cue. Fixed commitments are now tiny distant lights rather than physical poles.

## The transition

The first movement down the page advances toward the ridge. The sentence recedes, the terrain rises, and the narrower live horizon appears as the window above the desk. The transition is intentionally short; utility begins after one natural scroll.

## The Personal Desk

### Today

Current focus, next commitment, open time, the editorial agenda, plan health, and small entry points to the retained Plan and Calendar precision views.

### Projects

Four concise personal worlds: Ascendo, Cedrus, Play Nice, and Personal. Each opens a contextual environment with current work, next work, waiting items, people, recent changes, and a context-aware Ask field.

### People

Eight people shown only through shared context: current work, mutual waiting, next contact, and recent meaning. There are no scores, tiers, relationship warnings, or CRM mechanics.

### Briefs

Four recurring output structures with configurable mock inputs, sections, writing style, cadence, and delivery. The Newsletter includes a realistic generated-preview state. Delivery remains demo-only.

### Replay

A full-height chronology of twelve meaningful events. Scrolling moves the environmental time from morning toward sunset while preserving the reason behind plan changes and agent consequences.

## Universal layers

- Ask Cedrus and `⌘K` work across every section.
- Universal intake classifies a note into project, task, deadline, and suggested duration.
- The Cedrus wordmark opens transparent, editable personal knowledge.
- The hidden developer panel changes demo physics without appearing in normal use.

## Protected V6.2 boundary

`src/planner/`, `src/scenarios/`, and `tests/planner.test.ts` remain unchanged. Plan, Calendar, fixed/flexible semantics, rescheduling, Ask fact mutation, and agent consequence boundaries are retained.

## Local-only boundary

V6.3 uses realistic mock data and browser-local persistence only. It has no live provider connections, OAuth, backend, remote AI, or publishing.
