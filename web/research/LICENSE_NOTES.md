The visual-reference archives supplied with earlier Cedrus work were used only to identify interaction qualities such as spatial storytelling, cinematic transitions, and environmental composition. No reference-site HTML, CSS, JavaScript, imagery, fonts, or branding is included.

The prototype uses React, Vite, TypeScript, and Lucide icons under their published package licenses. Generated package metadata contains the exact dependency versions.
